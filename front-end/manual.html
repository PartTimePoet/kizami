How to kizami?

Meaning of kizami

Kizami (刻み) means "to carve a notch." Each notch is a small, complete act of focus. Stack enough of them together, and you've carved something real.

The Idea

Progress isn't one big swing — it's a thousand small cuts. Kizami is built for that. One task. One timer. One notch at a time. No fluff, no leaderboards, no gamification tricks. Just you and the work.

The Steps

  1. Set your task
  2. Run the timer 25 minutes. No pause button. No snooze. The timer runs until it's done.
  3. Carve a notch. Made it to the end? That's one kizami.
  4. Rest & repeat

Your Dashboard

  1. Notch count
  2. Streak
  3. Completion rate

A Few Ground Rules

  1. One notch, one session. Partial rounds don't count.
  2. Be honest with yourself.
  3. Streaks reset at midnight.
